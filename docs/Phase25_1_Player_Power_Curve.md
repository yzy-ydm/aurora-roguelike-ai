## 7. Floor1-Floor5 玩家DPS预测

### 假设条件

- 每层击杀怪物数：10
- 每层获得升级：2次
- 武器每层升级：1次
- 暴击率：10%

### DPS计算公式

```
DPS = (Player Attack + Weapon Damage) * (1 / Fire Rate) * (1 + Crit Rate * Crit Damage)
```

### Floor 1

| 参数 | 值 | 说明 |
|------|-----|------|
| Player Attack | 10 | 初始 |
| Weapon Damage | 20 | 初始 |
| Fire Rate | 0.2s | 5发/秒 |
| Crit Rate | 10% | 基础 |
| Crit Damage | 50% | 基础 |

**DPS = (10 + 20) * 5 * (1 + 0.1 * 0.5) = 150 * 1.05 = 157.5**

### Floor 2

| 参数 | 值 | 说明 |
|------|-----|------|
| Player Attack | 15 | +5 (升级) |
| Weapon Damage | 25 | +5 (武器升级) |
| Fire Rate | 0.2s | 5发/秒 |
| Crit Rate | 15% | +5% (升级) |
| Crit Damage | 50% | 基础 |

**DPS = (15 + 25) * 5 * (1 + 0.15 * 0.5) = 200 * 1.075 = 215**

### Floor 3

| 参数 | 值 | 说明 |
|------|-----|------|
| Player Attack | 20 | +10 |
| Weapon Damage | 30 | +10 |
| Fire Rate | 0.2s | 5发/秒 |
| Crit Rate | 20% | +10% |
| Crit Damage | 80% | +30% |

**DPS = (20 + 30) * 5 * (1 + 0.2 * 0.8) = 250 * 1.16 = 290**

### Floor 5

| 参数 | 值 | 说明 |
|------|-----|------|
| Player Attack | 30 | +20 |
| Weapon Damage | 40 | +20 |
| Fire Rate | 0.2s | 5发/秒 |
| Crit Rate | 25% | +15% |
| Crit Damage | 80% | +30% |

**DPS = (30 + 40) * 5 * (1 + 0.25 * 0.8) = 350 * 1.2 = 420**
